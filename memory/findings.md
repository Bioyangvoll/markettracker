# Product Scout Memory — Weekly Findings

## 2026-10-19
- Signal: Automatic pet feeders — search interest up 49% YoY (peaked at index 93 in May 2026); driven by multi-pet households and travel pet care
- Pain: All mainstream feeders are dry kibble-only; wet food causes blockages requiring daily disassembly; WiFi-dependent (cloud) scheduling causes missed meals during outages; most units only support 2.4GHz Wi-Fi

- Signal: Tallow-based skincare — hundreds of thousands of monthly searches; viral on Reddit and TikTok throughout 2025–2026
- Pain: Existing products work for dry/eczema-prone skin but clog pores for the much larger oily/combination-skin segment; dermatologists publicly warn against it for acne-prone users; no brand has produced a non-comedogenic or lighter-texture tallow formulation; FDA-unregulated with inconsistent quality

- Signal: Dog water fountains — 27,100 monthly searches as of July 2026, up 22% YoY
- Pain: Pump and filter disassembly for routine cleaning is the #1 recurring complaint; no mainstream fountain is fully dishwasher-safe or tool-free to completely disassemble; standing water in hard-to-reach pump housings breeds biofilm
